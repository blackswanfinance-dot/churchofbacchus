AN OPEN INVITATION

For freedom.

This church is for supporters of art & theatre. For people who care about personal freedom and equality. A community of people who are curious about the mysteries of life and death and who believe in the Tradition of Criticism. The ancient mysteries and rites of Dionysus were attended by similar people.

For a scholarly history of this tradition, explore Brian Muraresku’s discussion of the Mysteries of Eleusis on The Practical Stoic Podcast.

Society often limits “god” to the idea of an omnipotent person or deified prophet. This ignores how gods can represent our spiritual connection to the world, manifested as an idea form within us.

What is this experience? An openly mystical pursuit. We invite the audience to consider this powerful ancient tradition as a Portal, rather than thinking about some holy person you need to follow or compare yourself to.

The first and only official tenet of the church is, you do not talk about Church of Bacchus. Allow religious conversation to come up naturally. No proselytizing or buffeting people with your personal mystical beliefs.

Scope, range & source

Preliminary range: $175,000–350,000 for a 600–1,000-square-foot above-grade conditioned cellar fit-out. Cooling, insulation, racking, finishes, design and contingency are included.

How the recognition and the money will work

A contribution token is recognition, not a currency, investment, ownership share, repayment promise or automatic event ticket. The design has no trading, staking, interest or required token burning. Future privileges or event packages would have their own published terms.

The contribution record will show the original amount separately from fees, refunds and the amount available to the project. For example, a $100 gift with a hypothetical $3 processing cost leaves $97 for the chosen project. That is one $100 contribution, not $197 of funding. Refunded contributions must be corrected in the same record. No actual processor fee or live payment is implied by this example.

Donations can support approved work before a whole project is fully funded, including agreed planning and acquisition costs. A designated gift is not silently moved to another project. Permitted uses, changes of plan and unneeded balances will be agreed before accepting a project gift. There is no promise of a finished building by a particular date.

A verified stamp will require a confirmed payment record and a Church-issued signature. Choosing a project or visiting a payment page is not proof of a donation. Personal details and payment references should remain private. This browser sample is unsigned and cannot be redeemed.

The sample is not a tax receipt, and this page does not promise tax deductibility. Any future valuable benefits must be described and accounted for separately. See the IRS guidance on donation acknowledgments and benefits.

Principles

The Church of Bacchus holds individual autonomy as a primary good. Every person should possess the greatest possible freedom over their own body, mind, conscience, speech, associations, and peaceful conduct. We reject systems of unnecessary control and the presumption that any authority is entitled to rule another person merely because it possesses power.

We accept human fallibility as permanent. No ruler, institution, ideology, scripture, majority, expert, or tradition is beyond error. Therefore criticism must remain possible, dissent must remain protected, and institutions must be designed so that mistakes can be discovered and corrected without violence or catastrophe.

Political systems are tools rather than sacred objects. Their legitimacy depends on how well they preserve human autonomy, permit criticism, correct errors, and protect the conditions under which people can pursue flourishing lives.
