RU Child Safety Standards

Church of Bacchus, Inc. · Updated October 16, 2026

Standards against abuse and exploitation

RU prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including grooming, sexual exploitation, sextortion, trafficking of children, and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This includes sexualized depictions of minors in photographs, videos, illustrations or computer-generated imagery. RU must not be used to facilitate, encourage or promote this conduct.

How RU works

RU displays messages, animations and contact information on the user's own screen. It does not operate a matching service, public user profiles, an in-app chat network or a public content-upload service. Custom photos and messages remain on the user's device; they are not uploaded to Church servers. BitChat is a separate external product with its own services and policies.

These limits do not excuse abuse. These standards apply to RU's supplied content and the use of RU. We do not claim to monitor private devices or communications conducted through other services.

Report a child safety concern

Contact bacchuschurch@gmail.com with the subject RU child safety. This is the Church's designated RU child safety contact. In RU, the same support email is available under Options → Privacy & support, and from the Premium privacy/support link. No RU account or purchase is needed to access Options and the support contact.

Describe the concern, the relevant RU scene or feature, and any non-explicit reference or URL that helps us identify it. Do not send, forward or attach suspected CSAM, passwords or unnecessary personal details. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Reports about a separate service should also be made to that service.

Response and reporting

Church of Bacchus, Inc. reviews child safety concerns received at this contact. When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM or other child exploitation involving RU, we act to remove or disable content and access within our control, take steps to prevent recurrence, and comply with applicable reporting and preservation requirements. This includes reporting confirmed CSAM to the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children or the relevant regional authority as required by law.

RU has no hosted user-content feed or user messaging accounts to moderate or suspend. For content kept solely on a user's device or activity on a separate service, our technical control is limited. We still review reports relating to RU, address our own app and assets, and cooperate with the appropriate authorities as legally required.

Reports are handled with limited access. We seek only information necessary to assess and respond to the concern and retain it only as needed for that response and applicable legal obligations. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality or a particular outcome.
