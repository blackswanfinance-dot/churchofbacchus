RU Privacy Policy

Church of Bacchus, Inc. · Updated October 14, 2026

Privacy and support: bacchuschurch@gmail.com

What RU does

RU is a visual message and contact-display utility. It shows messages, animations and contact details on your own screen. RU does not create dating profiles, match people, search for partners or send RU-to-RU messages. No RU account is required.

This policy covers RU, including the Android app with package name com.areyou.mobile. It does not replace the policies of websites, app stores or apps you choose to open from RU.

Information kept on your device

Contact text you enter, display preferences, your Score collection and Custom stories, selected photos and saved Billboard settings are kept in the app's local storage. RU does not upload this content to the Church or to RevenueCat. Your contact text is visible to people who can see your screen; use only information you intend to display.

Custom uses the system file picker for photos you select. RU does not request access to your entire photo library, contacts, messages or precise location. Copies of selected photos are stored in the app's private storage. Original photos remain in the location from which you selected them.

A Quick Draw shortcut saves its scene selection. It reads your contact text from RU when opened rather than putting that text into the shortcut URL.

Microphone and live subtitles

When you choose to start live Subtitles and grant microphone access, RU passes microphone audio to your device or browser's speech-recognition service to turn speech into on-screen words. Depending on that service and its settings, audio may be sent to its servers for processing. Internet access may be required. RU does not record or save the audio, and does not upload audio or transcripts to a Church server.

Listening stops when you tap Stop, leave Subtitles or put RU in the background. The displayed transcript is temporary and can be cleared. Keyboard dictation is provided by your chosen keyboard and is subject to that provider's settings and privacy policy. You can type instead, without granting RU microphone access.

Premium purchases

The store version uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to sell, verify and restore the optional one-time Premium purchase. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated app user identifier, purchase tokens and transaction history, entitlement status, and technical information such as app/SDK version, platform and device information. These records let the app recognize a valid purchase and provide purchase support and purchase analytics. Your payment details are handled by Google Play; RU does not receive your card details.

Purchase-service traffic uses encrypted connections. RU does not request the Android advertising ID, include advertising SDKs or sell your personal data. A purchase-service identifier is not a public RU profile. Private field-test builds may bypass purchases; that testing access is not a store entitlement.

BitChat, websites and donations

BitChat is a separate product. RU only offers links that open BitChat, its official download pages or its website. RU does not collect a BitChat nickname, age, location, identity, room, message, participant list or moderation record. BitChat manages its own onboarding, permissions and communication under its own policies.

Opening the Church website, a support email or a Bitcoin wallet takes you outside RU. Those services receive the information you choose to give them and may process connection information under their own policies. Bitcoin transactions are public on the blockchain. A donation does not buy or unlock RU Premium.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Locally saved information remains until you edit or remove it, clear RU's storage or uninstall the app. Use the Custom deletion controls to remove saved stories/photos and Score's empty/reset control to clear that collection. On Android, Settings → Apps → RU → Storage & cache → Clear storage removes RU's local app data and preferences. This also removes saved Custom work and Score progress. It does not delete the original photos or revoke a Google Play purchase. Restore purchases can recover an eligible entitlement.

RU has no account to delete. For privacy questions or a request concerning purchase-service records, email bacchuschurch@gmail.com with the subject RU privacy. We may ask for a purchase reference to locate the correct records; do not send passwords, full payment details or wallet keys. We retain purchase/support records only as needed for entitlement management, support, security and applicable legal or accounting obligations. Deletion requests are assessed against those purposes and obligations; app-store transaction records remain subject to Google's policies.

You can revoke microphone permission in your device or browser settings. RU does not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information.

Security and updates

RU limits permissions to features you use, stores app content locally and disables Android app-data backup. No system can promise absolute privacy or security. Keep your device protected, and remember that anyone viewing your screen can read the information you display.

We update this policy when RU's data practices change. The date above identifies this version. Material changes involving new data use will also be explained in the app where appropriate.
