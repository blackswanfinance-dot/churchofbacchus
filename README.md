# Church of Bacchus

Static reconstruction from the owner's supplied Wix screenshots and original artwork. No packages, build process, Wix runtime, tracking scripts, or paid hosting dependency.

Development starts with [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and [CURRENT-STATE.md](CURRENT-STATE.md).
They connect this repository to the shared Chief of Bots Airtable work history;
current tasks and decisions stay there rather than in a second local queue.

## Project support and contribution stamps — September 28, 2026

The public `#grounds` section presents five designated projects with concept illustrations, sourced planning ranges and exclusions: land ($4.5m working allowance), theatre ($8m), cellar ($250k), trail/labyrinth ($300k) and a travelling pay-what-you-can food truck ($250k startup plus first year). Combined working figure $13.3m is not a complete campus price; major shared site works, carrying/collection costs and future operation remain additional. Land is not owned and no parcel is selected. No verified project fundraising totals are displayed.

The `#contribution` section demonstrates an unsigned project stamp. No vesting, fundraising lock, priced token units, speculative circulation or investment rights. Choosing a project and example amount changes the sample and composes an email inquiry; it never proves a payment, sends an email, submits a form, collects personal information or issues a token. The email asks the Church to confirm designation and terms before money is sent. Existing PayPal and Bitcoin links are clearly general support and do not inherit the project selection.

Food truck concept: visit selected neighbourhoods with a prominent voluntary donation sign supporting the Church. Meals do not require a gift. First-year costs assume zero uncommitted roadside receipts. Keep actual restricted food support and general Church support separate in any future ledger.

Actual contribution recognition requires reconciled payments, a Church-controlled issuer/signature, correction handling and a durable private ledger/verification service. None is deployed here. The public sample must remain visibly unsigned, not issued, and not a tax receipt. A completed-project endorsement cannot claim a building exists before completion. No old laboratory contracts, ticket demos or private RU builds were copied into this public repository.

Validation: `node --test tests/grounds.test.mjs` checks exact cents, invalid input, project uniqueness, the budget sum and correctly encoded inquiry amounts. Browser checks passed at 1440, 390 and 320px, plus no-JavaScript access: 50 checks, no page errors, overflow or write requests. Desktop/mobile screenshots reviewed; screenshots stay ignored in `test-results/`. Existing donation URLs, contact, quotations and CNAME preserved.
